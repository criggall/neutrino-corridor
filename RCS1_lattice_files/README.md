This folder contains the RCS1 lattice files provided by Kyle. His description of the files:

"Here are the Bmad files for the "multi-superperiod units" of the RCS 1 at reference energy (ref.bmad), injection energy (inj.bmad), and extraction energy (ext.bmad) as well as the init files (tao.init and beam_mod.init) that configure Bmad and Tao."
